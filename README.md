# Indian Food Delivery Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

This Power BI project analyzes the performance of India's leading food delivery platforms—Zomato, Swiggy, and Dunzo—across multiple cities, restaurants, cuisines, and customer segments. The dashboard provides comprehensive insights into revenue, order volume, customer behavior, delivery efficiency, restaurant performance, and sales trends.

### Key Highlights

* Analyzed **24.36M+ total revenue** generated from **28,000+ orders** across 7 major Indian cities.
* Compared business performance of **Zomato, Swiggy, and Dunzo** using revenue, market share, ratings, and delivery metrics.
* Identified **Pizza** as the highest revenue-generating cuisine and **Delhi** as the top-performing city.
* Evaluated customer demographics, payment preferences, age-group spending patterns, and customer segmentation.
* Developed restaurant analytics including revenue contribution, ratings analysis, cuisine performance, and top restaurant rankings.
* Tracked monthly sales trends, order volume fluctuations, delivery success rates, and operational KPIs.

### Tools & Technologies

### Business Value

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor platform performance, understand customer behavior, identify high-performing cuisines and cities, optimize delivery operations, and support data-driven business decisions in the food delivery industry.
